              # LedgerBud AI — Financial Advisor RAG
              
### Project Summary:

  - LedgerBud AI is an AI-powered personal finance assistant that helps users understand, analyze, and optimize their spending habits using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

  - Users can upload bank statements, transaction histories, and expense records, and interact with the system through natural language. Instead of manually reviewing hundreds of transactions, users can ask questions such as:

Why did my expenses increase this month?
Where am I overspending?
Which spending categories should I reduce?
How much did I spend on food over the last six months?
What are my recurring subscriptions?

The system processes financial documents, categorizes transactions, generates embeddings, and stores them in a vector database. When a user asks a question, the RAG pipeline retrieves relevant financial records and provides context-aware answers, spending insights, trend analysis, and personalized budgeting recommendations.

Beyond simple expense tracking, LedgerBud AI acts as a financial intelligence platform by identifying spending patterns, detecting unusual expenses, forecasting future spending trends, and suggesting actionable ways to improve financial health.
